# Agility Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Agility is a desktop PDF editor published by swyhart. This policy explains what Agility does with your information. In short: Agility works entirely on your computer and does not collect, send or sell any of your data.

## What we collect

Nothing. Agility has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. We never receive your documents, your signatures or any information about how you use the app.

## Your documents

Every PDF and Office file you open is read, edited, converted and saved on your own computer. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your computer too, using language data that ships with the app. Agility never uploads a document anywhere.

## What Agility stores on your computer

To work properly, Agility keeps a few files in its own app data folder. They never leave your computer.

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your 12 most recently opened files | To show the Open Recent list |
| Preferences, such as the theme | To remember your settings |
| Up to 8 saved signatures (typed or drawn) | So you can reuse them |
| Digital-signing certificates you create or import | To sign documents. Each is stored in a password-protected file (AES-256). |
| A list of fonts installed on your computer | To match fonts when you edit text |
| The files that were open when you last closed Agility | To reopen them the next time you start it |
| Recovery copies of documents with unsaved changes | To restore your work after a crash. They're deleted when you save, discard or close the document. |

In the Microsoft Store version, this folder is removed when you uninstall Agility. You can also clear your recent files, signatures and certificates from inside the app at any time.

## Internet access

The Microsoft Store version of Agility makes no network connections on its own. Windows and the Microsoft Store handle its updates.

Agility goes online only when you ask it to. If you click a web link inside a PDF, it opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies. When Agility imports Word or Excel files, it blocks any remote images or content they link to.

If you tick *Add a trusted timestamp* when signing with a certificate, Agility contacts the timestamp server shown in that dialog (DigiCert's public server unless you change it) so it can confirm the signing time. It sends only a fingerprint (hash) of the signature, never the document or anything about you. The option is off unless you turn it on.

The versions of Agility downloaded outside the Microsoft Store check GitHub for new releases. That check sends only the standard information any web request includes, such as your IP address, to GitHub, and Agility receives nothing about you in return.

## Children

Agility collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If Agility's handling of data ever changes, we'll update this policy and its effective date before the change ships.
